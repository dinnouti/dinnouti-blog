# Perfect Pancake Recipe

Pancakes are a classic American breakfast that are light, fluffy, and absolutely delicious. They're perfect for a weekend breakfast or brunch with family and friends. This recipe yields about 12 medium-sized pancakes.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon white sugar
- 1 1/4 cups milk
- 1 egg
- 3 tablespoons butter, melted

## Instructions

1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the dry ingredients: flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.

2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk, egg, and melted butter.

3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. The batter should be slightly lumpy - don't overmix!

4. Let the batter rest for 5-10 minutes while you heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat.

5. Lightly grease the skillet with butter or oil.

6. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter onto the skillet for each pancake.

7. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and the edges start to look set, about 2-3 minutes.

8. Flip and cook for another 1-2 minutes on the other side until golden brown.

9. Serve immediately with your favorite toppings - I recommend fresh fruit, whipped cream, or maple syrup.

## Tips

- For extra fluffy pancakes, you can separate the egg. Add the yolk with the other wet ingredients, then beat the egg white to soft peaks and fold it into the batter at the end.
- If the batter seems too thick, you can add a little more milk, one tablespoon at a time, until you reach the desired consistency.
- Keep cooked pancakes warm in a 200°F (95°C) oven while you finish the batch.

## Mix-in Variations

- **Blueberry**: fold in 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries (if frozen, don't thaw first)
- **Chocolate chip**: fold in 1/2-3/4 cup chocolate chips
- **Banana**: mash 1 ripe banana and add to wet ingredients, reduce milk by 1/4 cup
- **Apple cinnamon**: fold in 1/2 cup finely diced apple and 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- **Veggie**: add 1/2 cup grated zucchini (squeezed dry) or pumpkin puree with warming spices

Enjoy your delicious homemade pancakes!
